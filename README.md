**🌐 Overview
**AAROGYA-47** is a next-generation, futuristic clinical intelligence platform built for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2024, Problem Statement #47**. It transforms India's most critical healthcare pain point — **chaotic, paper-based, 30-page patient case records** — into a **single, high-density, actionable 1-page clinical cockpit** for every Indian doctor, ASHA worker, and OPD patient.
Designed to work from the most advanced urban hospitals to the most remote **Ayushman Arogya Mandirs** in rural India — in 6+ vernacular languages, with zero paper.

**🎯 Problem Statement
> **PS #47 — Patient Case Taking System**
> India's OPDs handle **10 crore+ outpatients monthly**. Doctors spend 40-60% of consultation time decoding old prescriptions, handwritten notes, and scattered lab reports. Rural patients with **no documents** have no accessible case history. Fragmented records lead to wrong dosages, missed drug allergies, repeated investigations, and preventable deaths.
### The Specific Pain Points:
| Pain | Scale |
|------|-------|
| 📄 Paper-based records per patient | 30–100+ pages |
| ⏱️ Time doctors spend on paperwork | 40–60% of consultation |
| 🌾 Rural patients with zero records | ~600 Million |
| 💊 Drug errors due to missing allergy data | 1.3M+ annually |
| 🔁 Repeated investigations (duplicate) | 35% avoidable |

## ✨ Key Features
### 🔐 ABHA / ABDM Gateway
- 14-digit **ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account)** login
- Aadhaar OTP-based Federated Authentication
- Instant sync with **ABDM National Health Locker** — pulls all prior FHIR records
- Digital **Ayushman Bharat Health Card** hologram with QR
### 📄 Option 1 — Smart Document Vision OCR Scanner
- Simulated **laser sweep scanner** with animated confidence bounding boxes
- Extracts: Diagnosis, Prescription Drugs (Name, Dose, Frequency), Doctor signature
- 97.4% OCR confidence scoring on handwritten prescriptions
- Patient **reviews and confirms** extracted data before it enters the timeline
### 🎙️ Option 2 — Vernacular Voice Case-Taking
- **Real-time microphone** with animated soundwave visualizer
- Captures speech in **Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali** and more
- Neural STT engine with **Live Clinical English Translation**
- Auto-extracts all **11 clinical parameters**:
  1. First Symptoms
  2. First Consultations
  3. Diagnosis
  4. Medicines Taken
  5. Investigations / Tests
  6. Hospitalizations
  7. Previous Prescriptions Attached
  8. Previous Lab Reports
  9. Current Status
  10. Current Symptoms
  11. Known Allergies
  
### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Verification & Override — Human-in-the-Loop
- AI drafts are **never auto-committed** without human validation
- Doctor views AI-extracted clinical impression and Rx
- Doctor can **override** any field: Diagnosis, Dosages, Remarks
- One-click **Cryptographic NMC Sign-Off** — SHA-256 tamper-proof hash stamp
- Verification status locked to patient ABHA record
### 🖥️ 1-Page High-Density Clinical Cockpit
- Replaces **30+ pages of paper chaos** with 1 beautifully structured page
- **Interactive Holographic Organ Telemetry Map** — clickable organ hotspots (Pancreas, Eyes, Nerves, Heart, Kidneys, Lungs)
- Active Pharmacotherapy Regimen with dose/frequency/duration
- Allergy Banner with critical drug warnings
- AYUSH Integrative Health Plan suggestions
- **PDF Export** — Official 1-Page clinical executive summary printout
### 📅 Lifetime Unified Medical Timeline
- Chronological scrubber of all health events from birth to present
- Integrates: OCR scans, voice intakes, hospital admissions, specialist visits, investigations
- FHIR-aligned event classification
### 🏛️ Government Scheme Intelligence Engine
- Auto-matches patient profile to eligible schemes:
  - **PM-JAY** → ₹5,00,000/year (pmjay.gov.in)
  - **National AYUSH Mission** (ayush.gov.in)
  - **State Schemes** — Mukhyamantri Jan Arogya (UP), CMCHIS (Tamil Nadu)
- One-click **Pre-Authorization check** simulation
- Direct links to official government portals
### 🔒 DISHA & HIPAA Data Privacy Module
- ABDM **Consent Manager** — toggleable access grants per doctor/duration
- Emergency 24x7 Break-Glass access
**AES-256 End-to-End Encryption** logs
- Immutable **DISHA Cryptographic Audit Trail** with SHA-3 hashes per action
- Role-Based Access Control (Patient / Doctor / ASHA Worker)
